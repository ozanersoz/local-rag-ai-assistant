# South America Political Regions and Organizations

Source basis: paraphrased notes from Wikipedia pages about South America, Mercosur, Pacific Alliance, Andean Community, Union of South American Nations, and country pages.

South America includes sovereign states such as Argentina, Bolivia, Brazil, Chile, Colombia, Ecuador, Guyana, Paraguay, Peru, Suriname, Uruguay, and Venezuela. French Guiana is not a sovereign state; it is an overseas department and region of France.

The Southern Cone generally refers to the southern part of South America and commonly includes Argentina, Chile, and Uruguay, with Paraguay and southern Brazil often included in broader regional discussion.

The Andean region refers to countries shaped by the Andes, especially Colombia, Ecuador, Peru, Bolivia, Chile, Argentina, and Venezuela. The term can mean different things depending on political, geographic, or cultural context.

The Guianas region includes Guyana, Suriname, French Guiana, and nearby northern South American areas. Guyana is English-speaking, Suriname is Dutch-speaking, and French Guiana is connected politically to France.

Mercosur and the Pacific Alliance are examples of regional economic organizations that connect South American countries to trade and diplomatic networks. The Andean Community is another regional organization associated with Andean countries.
