# South American Country Profiles

Source basis: paraphrased country facts from Wikipedia country pages for Argentina, Bolivia, Brazil, Chile, Colombia, Ecuador, Guyana, Paraguay, Peru, Suriname, Uruguay, and Venezuela.

Argentina is a country in the Southern Cone of South America. Its capital and largest city is Buenos Aires. Argentina is the second-largest country in South America by area after Brazil. It borders Chile, Bolivia, Paraguay, Brazil, Uruguay, the South Atlantic Ocean, and the Drake Passage. Argentina is organized as a federal state with provinces and an autonomous capital city.

Bolivia is a landlocked country in central South America. Sucre is the constitutional capital, while La Paz is the seat of government. Bolivia borders Brazil, Paraguay, Argentina, Chile, and Peru. Its geography includes Andean highlands, the Altiplano, valleys, tropical lowlands, Amazonian areas, and part of the Pantanal wetland.

Brazil is the largest country in South America and one of the largest countries in the world by both area and population. Its capital is Brasília, and major cities include São Paulo and Rio de Janeiro. Brazil is the only Portuguese-speaking country in the Americas, and it has the world's largest Portuguese-speaking population.

Chile is a long, narrow country along the western edge of South America between the Andes Mountains and the Pacific Ocean. Its capital and largest city is Santiago. Chile borders Peru, Bolivia, Argentina, and the Drake Passage. It also administers Pacific islands, including Easter Island.

Colombia is located in northwestern South America and also has Caribbean and Pacific coastlines. Its capital and largest city is Bogotá. Colombia borders Venezuela, Brazil, Peru, Ecuador, and Panama. It is divided into departments and has major urban centers such as Medellín, Cali, Barranquilla, Cartagena, and Bucaramanga.

Ecuador is a country in northwestern South America. It borders Colombia, Peru, and the Pacific Ocean. Quito is the capital, while Guayaquil is the largest city. Ecuador also includes the Galápagos Islands, located in the Pacific Ocean west of the mainland.

Guyana is on the northern coast of South America and is part of the Guianas region. Its capital and largest city is Georgetown. Guyana borders the Atlantic Ocean, Suriname, Brazil, and Venezuela. It is one of the English-speaking countries of South America.

Paraguay is a landlocked country in central South America. Its capital and largest city is Asunción. Paraguay borders Bolivia, Brazil, and Argentina. Although it has no ocean coastline, it connects to the Atlantic through the Paraná-Paraguay Waterway.

Peru is a country in western South America. Its capital and largest city is Lima. Peru borders Ecuador, Colombia, Brazil, Bolivia, Chile, and the Pacific Ocean. The country is megadiverse, with coastal deserts, Andean mountains, and Amazon rainforest regions.

Suriname is a country on the northern coast of South America and is also culturally connected to the Caribbean. Its capital and largest city is Paramaribo. Suriname borders the Atlantic Ocean, French Guiana, Brazil, and Guyana. It is the smallest sovereign country in South America by area and population, and much of its territory is covered by rainforest.

Uruguay is a country in southeastern South America and part of the Southern Cone. Its capital and largest city is Montevideo. Uruguay borders Argentina, Brazil, the Río de la Plata, and the Atlantic Ocean. A large share of its population lives in the Montevideo metropolitan area.

Venezuela is located on the northern coast of South America and includes mainland territory plus Caribbean islands and islets. Its capital and largest urban area is Caracas. Venezuela borders Colombia, Brazil, Guyana, the Caribbean Sea, and the Atlantic Ocean. Its geography includes the Andes, Caribbean coast, Llanos plains, the Orinoco region, and areas of the Guiana Highlands.
