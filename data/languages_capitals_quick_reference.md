# South America Capitals and Languages Quick Reference

Argentina: capital Buenos Aires; official/national language Spanish.

Bolivia: constitutional capital Sucre; seat of government La Paz; Spanish and Indigenous languages are important in national life.

Brazil: capital Brasília; official language Portuguese.

Chile: capital Santiago; official/national language Spanish.

Colombia: capital Bogotá; official language Spanish, with other languages recognized regionally.

Ecuador: capital Quito; largest city Guayaquil; official language Spanish, with Indigenous languages also important.

Guyana: capital Georgetown; official language English.

Paraguay: capital Asunción; Spanish and Guaraní are widely associated with national identity.

Peru: capital Lima; Spanish is widely used, and Indigenous languages such as Quechua and Aymara are important.

Suriname: capital Paramaribo; Dutch is the official language.

Uruguay: capital Montevideo; Spanish is the main language.

Venezuela: capital Caracas; Spanish is the official language, and Indigenous languages are recognized.
