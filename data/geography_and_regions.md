# South America Geography and Regions

The Andes Mountains shape western South America and run through or along countries such as Colombia, Ecuador, Peru, Bolivia, Chile, Argentina, and Venezuela. Countries along the Pacific side include Colombia, Ecuador, Peru, and Chile. Countries with Atlantic coastlines include Venezuela, Guyana, Suriname, Brazil, Uruguay, and Argentina.

The Amazon Basin covers large parts of northern and central South America, especially Brazil, Peru, Colombia, Bolivia, Ecuador, Venezuela, and the Guianas. The basin is important for biodiversity, rivers, rainforest ecosystems, and climate regulation.

The Southern Cone generally includes Argentina, Chile, Uruguay, Paraguay, and southern Brazil. Argentina and Chile share the southern part of the continent, while Uruguay lies southeast of Brazil and east of Argentina.

Landlocked South American countries include Bolivia and Paraguay. Bolivia has highland and lowland regions, while Paraguay connects to wider trade routes through inland river systems.

The Guianas region includes Guyana, Suriname, French Guiana, and nearby parts of northern South America. Guyana and Suriname are sovereign countries; French Guiana is an overseas department and region of France.
